What this website does — and does not do

This homepage has no enquiry form, accounts or log-ins. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or social media tracking, and it does not load fonts or scripts from third-party services. Our “Email” buttons simply open your own email application; nothing is sent until you choose to send it.

Information we receive

  • When you visit: our hosting provider’s servers automatically process technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the website and keep it secure.
  • When you email or call us: your name, contact details, business details and anything you choose to tell us, plus any later correspondence and notes we make to deal with your enquiry.

Why we use it and our lawful basis

We use this information to deliver and protect the website, respond to your enquiry, discuss whether we can help, and manage any resulting business relationship. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interests in running our business and responding to people who contact us, or taking steps at your request before entering into a contract. Where we must keep records by law, we rely on legal obligation. We do not sell your information or add you to marketing lists because you contacted us.

Who processes it for us

  • Email: Google Workspace (provided by Google), which receives, stores and sends email for our hclcommercial.co.uk mailbox.
  • Website hosting: TO CONFIRM (item 2): hosting provider, which processes technical request data to serve the website.
  • Telephone: TO CONFIRM (item 5): telephone service provider, and whether calls are recorded

We may also share information with professional advisers, or where required by law.

We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry. If no business relationship follows, we delete it after TO CONFIRM (item 3): retention period.
