Privacy notice

HCL Commercial website and direct enquiries. Last updated: publication date — set on the day the site goes live

Who is responsible for your information

What this website does — and does not do

This website has no enquiry form, accounts or log-ins. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or social media tracking, and it does not load fonts or scripts from third-party services. Our email buttons simply open your own email application; nothing is sent until you choose to send it.

Information we receive

  • When you visit: our hosting provider’s servers automatically process technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the website and keep it secure.
  • When you email us: your name, email address, business details and anything you choose to tell us, plus any later correspondence.
  • When you call us: your telephone number (unless withheld), what you tell us during the call and any notes we make to deal with your enquiry. Calls are not recorded.

Why we use it and our lawful basis

We use this information to deliver and protect the website, respond to your enquiry, discuss whether we can help, and manage any resulting business relationship. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interests in running our business and responding to people who contact us, or taking steps at your request before entering into a contract. Where we must keep records by law, we rely on legal obligation. We do not sell your information or add you to marketing lists because you contacted us.

Who processes it for us

  • Website hosting: Netlify, Inc., which hosts this website and processes technical request data, such as IP addresses, to serve and secure it.
  • Email: Google Workspace, provided by Google, which receives, stores and sends email for our hclcommercial.co.uk mailbox.
  • Telephone: our telephone service provider, which connects calls and keeps the call records needed to provide its service.

We may also share information with professional advisers, or where required by law.

Processing outside the UK

Netlify and Google may process personal information outside the UK, including in the United States.

  • Netlify: its Data Processing Agreement, which forms part of its customer terms, provides that transfers of UK personal data are made under the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses as amended by the UK International Data Transfer Addendum issued by the Information Commissioner’s Office.
  • Google: for transfers of UK personal data to the United States, Google relies on the UK Extension to the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, to which Google LLC has certified, as set out in its Cloud Data Processing Addendum and published transfer information.

Contact us if you would like more information about these arrangements.

How long we keep it

We do not currently apply a fixed retention period to enquiries. We keep enquiry emails and call notes only for as long as they are needed to respond to you and to manage any business relationship that follows, and for longer only where we need to meet legal, tax or accounting obligations or to establish or defend legal claims. You can ask us to delete your enquiry at any time.

Our hosting provider keeps its technical server logs in line with its own log-retention periods.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, restrict or object to how we use it, or, where applicable, ask for it to be transferred. Email barry@hclcommercial.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Scope

This notice covers this website and direct enquiries to HCL Commercial. If we provide a service that involves different processing, we will give you separate privacy information for it.
