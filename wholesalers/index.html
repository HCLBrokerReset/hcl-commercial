Sound familiar?

Most wholesalers know there are more customers out there. Finding time to win them is the hard part.

  • Growth relies on existing relationships

    Most new business comes through people you already know. It works — until a key account slows down and there is nothing in the pipeline to replace it.

  • New retailers and trade customers are hard to find

    Independent hardware shops, garden centres, merchants and trade counters are spread out, and working out which ones suit your ranges and delivery area takes time.

  • Prospecting happens in bursts

    Someone makes a push when it goes quiet, then the warehouse gets busy and it stops. Prospects hear from you once and never again.

  • Follow-up slips

    Interested buyers ask for a price list or a catalogue, and nobody gets back to them at the right moment. Good opportunities quietly go cold.

  • Owners and reps have too little time

    The people best placed to win new accounts are also running buying, stock, deliveries and existing customers. Growth work is always tomorrow’s job.

How it works

A steady, organised business-development process — run for you.

  1. 01

    Agree who you want to win

    We start with your ranges, delivery area, minimum order, capacity and best existing customers, and agree a clear profile of the retailers and trade buyers worth pursuing.

  2. 02

    Identify and research prospects

    We build a focused list of businesses that match that profile, and check each one for relevance — what they sell, where they are and whether your ranges make sense for them.

  3. 03

    Prepare targeted approaches

    We draft introductions that speak to each type of buyer and explain what you offer them. You approve the wording and the target list before anything goes out.

  4. 04

    Manage consistent follow-up

    Approaches are followed up on an agreed rhythm by email and telephone, so promising conversations are kept moving and nothing is left hanging.

  5. 05

    Hand over interested opportunities

    When a buyer is interested, your team receives an organised handover: who they are, what they are interested in, what has been discussed and the agreed next step.

What this is — and isn’t

An extension of your sales effort, with you in control.

What you get

  • Business development carried out for you, consistently, week after week
  • Prospects researched for relevance, not scraped from a list
  • Approaches and target lists you approve before they are used
  • Organised handovers your team can act on straight away
  • Regular updates on what has been done and where each opportunity stands

What it isn’t

  • A bought list of leads
  • Marketing software or an AI chatbot
  • Mass, untargeted emailing
  • A replacement for your sales team — pricing, account opening and orders stay with you

All outreach is business-to-business, makes clear who it is from and respects requests not to be contacted again.

Who it suits

Independent wholesalers ready to take on more accounts.

This service is designed for independent UK wholesalers and distributors supplying the DIY, hardware, building-supplies, garden, decorating, ironmongery and related trades — businesses with good ranges and the capacity to serve more customers, but without the time or people to pursue them consistently.

It is likely to be a good fit if:

  • You want more independent retailers or trade customers in your delivery area
  • Most of your new business currently comes from existing relationships
  • You can take on new accounts without major changes to stock or logistics
  • You would rather your team spent their time serving customers than chasing them

Questions

Straight answers.

Do you sell lists of leads?

No. We identify and research prospects specifically for your business, approach them and follow up. The work is in finding the right buyers and turning interest into a conversation with your team.

Will you contact businesses without our approval?

No. You approve the target profile, the list and the wording of approaches before anything is sent, including how we introduce ourselves on your behalf.

Does this replace our sales team or reps?

No. It gives them a steady flow of researched, interested prospects. Pricing, terms, account opening and orders stay with your business.

Can you guarantee new accounts?

No — and anyone who does should be treated with caution. What we commit to is consistent, well-targeted activity, organised follow-up and clear updates on where every opportunity stands.

What does it cost?

It depends on the scope — the number and type of prospects, the area covered and the level of follow-up. We agree the scope and costs with you in writing before any work starts.
