What we collect

We collect the information you give us: your name, care home or group name, number of homes, email address, renewal month and, if supplied, telephone number. We also keep relevant correspondence and records of the introduction and subsequent relationship. If you give us another person's business contact details, please make sure they know you are doing so and give them this notice.

The website and form providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details and request logs to deliver and protect their services. Please do not send resident information, medical information, claims details or insurance documents through this initial enquiry form. Insurance fact-finding takes place with your introduced broker.

Why we use it and our lawful basis

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, confirm whether our current service is relevant, coordinate a requested introduction, maintain contact about that introduction and manage the relationship. We also use necessary records to handle complaints, protect the service from misuse and establish or defend legal claims.

Our proposed lawful basis is legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) of the UK GDPR. These interests are providing the introduction and relationship support you request, operating our business and protecting our service and legal rights. We limit use to what is necessary and balance these interests against your privacy and reasonable expectations. Where a specific legal obligation requires us to use or retain information, we rely on Article 6(1)(c) for that use.

Providing information is voluntary, with no statutory requirement to complete this form. The required fields enable us to respond and coordinate an introduction; without sufficient details we may be unable to do so. Telephone is optional. Submitting an enquiry does not subscribe you to an unrelated marketing mailing list.

Broker introductions and other recipients

We will tell you which broker we propose and confirm the next steps with you before passing on your details. We share only the business contact and enquiry information needed for that introduction. You can tell us not to proceed. The broker is responsible for its own insurance advice, fact-finding, quotations and placement and will provide its own privacy information for its processing.

Formspree processes submitted enquiries and sends notifications to HCL. Our contact mailbox is provided through 123 Reg. The intended website host is GitHub Pages. These providers and their relevant service suppliers process information needed to deliver, secure and support those services. We may also disclose necessary information to professional advisers or public authorities where required by law or necessary to deal with legal claims. We do not sell your contact details as a marketing list.

How long we keep information

For an unsuccessful enquiry, we delete the enquiry record 12 months after the last contact, unless an ongoing relationship or another documented reason requires retention. This applies to HCL-controlled copies in email, the form archive and any enquiry records.

For an ongoing relationship, we keep relevant contact and introduction records while needed to provide relationship support and manage associated referral records. We review whether they remain necessary when the relationship ends. Where a legal duty, complaint or legal claim requires longer retention, we keep only the information needed for that purpose and delete it when the reason no longer applies. A broker applies its own retention rules to its records.

Website storage, cookies and security

This website does not use analytics, advertising pixels or tracking embeds. Its code does not set cookies or retain enquiry details in browser storage. Hosting and form providers may keep operational and security logs. When you use an email link, it opens your own email application and you decide whether to send the message. External websites have their own privacy information.

Form submissions use an HTTPS connection to Formspree. No internet or email service can guarantee absolute security. Access to HCL enquiry records should be limited to people who need them for the purposes above.

Your rights and complaints

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask for access to your information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion or restriction of use. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. Data portability applies where its legal conditions are met; it does not generally apply to processing based only on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw that consent without affecting earlier lawful use.

Contact us at barry@hclcommercial.co.uk to exercise your rights. We may need proportionate information to verify your identity. We will respond within the time required by data protection law and explain any applicable exception. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/.
